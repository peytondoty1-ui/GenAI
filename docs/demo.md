## Natural 2–3 minute script

Target: approximately 2 minutes 40 seconds, depending on narration and clicks. The timing is a recording plan, not a measured test result.

**Opening — show the notice and title**

“This is LedgerProof AI, a course prototype for a tax associate at a regional CPA firm preparing a draft memo for professional review. Everything here uses fictional examples and prepared responses. This is a Wizard-of-Oz simulation: it does not research tax law or decide whether a deduction is allowed.”

**Complete example — load and generate**

“I’ll start with Northstar’s laptop purchase. The draft separates facts, the tax question, the purchase-total calculation, and the unresolved conclusion. Four laptops at eighteen hundred dollars each gives a seventy-two-hundred-dollar purchase total. That is not a verified deductible amount.”

**Inspect support — open one invoice source and one reported-statement source**

“The important facts have inspectable support. Here the invoice is a fictional documentary sample. The service date and business-use statement come from a fictional client questionnaire, not independent verification. A service date in 2026 does not establish the client's tax year, which still needs confirmation in this example. The review checklist is a workflow aid, not authoritative tax guidance.”

**Missing-facts continuation — load missing, generate, load prepared answers, generate**

“The earlier version asked for missing information but rejected the follow-up answers. Now I can load the missing-facts example, see what is needed, and supply these clearly labeled prepared Northstar answers. The updated draft uses those client-reported answers. It does not pretend that an invoice was supplied. Policy, elections, research, and professional approval remain unresolved.”

**Draft control — edit working notes, save, inspect review items**

“I can edit draft working notes—for example, request the missing election documents—without changing the protected provisional status or implying approval. I can inspect the remaining review items. If this draft is unsuitable, I can reject it. Changing client facts invalidates the old result.”

**Safety check — load conflicting figures**

“When the example reports an eight-thousand-dollar invoice total against four laptops at eighteen hundred dollars each, the app asks for clarification rather than silently accepting the total. Unsupported requests to claim a verified full deduction do not produce an approved memo.”

**Close — show repository documentation**

“The repository documents the same-task before and after, five distinct browser tests, limitations, and an over-reliance risk. These checks support a limited workflow claim, not tax accuracy or measured time savings. Next steps are reviewer evaluation with fictional tasks and a carefully scoped evidence intake—not autonomous tax advice.”
