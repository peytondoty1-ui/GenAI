# LedgerProof prototype revision demo

This transcript accompanies an actual screen recording of the prototype. Narration is generated with the built-in macOS Samantha voice. It does not imitate Peyton's voice. Product drafting and research are separately disclosed as simulated.

## 1. intro

This is LedgerProof, a course prototype for tax associates at regional accounting firms. It organizes fictional client facts into a provisional memo for professional review. This screen recording uses computer generated narration. The research and drafting behavior inside the product is also simulated.

## 2. complete

The central task starts with client facts. In this complete example, Northstar purchased four laptops at one thousand eight hundred dollars each. Generating the draft organizes the facts, issue, purchase total, evidence, and unresolved review questions. Seven thousand two hundred dollars is the purchase total, not a verified deduction.

## 3. missing

The main revision addresses peer feedback about missing information. Previously, the prototype asked for more facts, but supplying answers led to a message saying that only two examples were supported. Here, the missing facts example asks for the information needed to continue. It does not guess the missing details.

## 4. followup

Now the user can supply the requested answers and continue to an updated provisional draft. The policy and election documents are still missing, so those questions remain unresolved. Completing the workflow does not mean that the software has researched the law or approved the tax treatment.

## 5. evidence

The second improvement makes the support easier to inspect. Important facts point back to fictional sample evidence. Invoice information is separated from client reported use and service dates. The review checklist is explicitly identified as a workflow aid, rather than authoritative tax guidance.

## 6. review

The third improvement gives the associate more control over the draft. The user can add working notes or reject an unsuitable result. Core facts, the unresolved conclusion, and provisional status remain protected. Editing input facts invalidates the old result, so a memo based on earlier information is not silently carried forward.

## 7. tests

The evaluation covers two typical tasks, conflicting figures, empty input, and a request for an unsupported definitive deduction. Each case records the expected behavior, the observed result, and the next implication. These tests assess this limited simulated experience. They do not establish model accuracy, tax correctness, or measured time savings.

## 8. limits

The main risk is over reliance on a draft that looks authoritative. The response combines visible limits with clarification and review controls. Professional judgment and authoritative research are still required. Peyton chose to keep the AI simulated for this milestone. The next priority is testing the workflow with tax associates, before expanding its scope.
